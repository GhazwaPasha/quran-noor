Privacy policy

Quran Noor · Last updated 12 November 2026

Quran Noor is a Quran reading and listening app. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and what you can do about it. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

Your account

You sign in with Google or with an email and password. From your account we receive your email address, your name and, for Google sign-in, your profile picture link. The picture itself is shown from Google's link and is not copied or stored by us.

What is saved to your account

So your app looks the same on every device, these are saved to your account:

  • Reading progress, bookmarks and history.
  • App settings, such as theme, text size, reciter, translation and listening choices, and prayer-time calculation settings.
  • The place used for prayer times: its name and its approximate coordinates.
  • Adhkar and practices you create, and your reminder times.

This data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, and only your own signed-in account can read or change it.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your device's location to work out prayer times and the qibla direction. Only the resulting place for prayer times is saved, as described above. Your location is not tracked in the background and is not shared with anyone.

Photos and camera

You can take or choose a photo of a dua so the app can read its text. The text is read on your device, and the photo is deleted straight away; it is never uploaded or kept. Only the text you review and save is kept.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules the azan and your reminders on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Quran text and audio

Quran text, translations and recitations are downloaded from public sources, such as Tanzil, EveryAyah, Al Quran Cloud and Islamic Network. Those requests contain only what is needed to fetch the file, like any web request, and no account information.

Deleting your data

You can sign out at any time. You can ask for your account and everything saved to it to be deleted through the developer contact on the app's Google Play listing. Deleting an account removes all of its saved data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from children beyond what is described here.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
