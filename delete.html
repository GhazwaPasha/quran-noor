Delete your account and data

Quran Noor

In the app

  1. Open Quran Noor and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and everything saved to it are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email ghazwairshad@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Quran Noor account”, from the email address you signed in with. Your account and its data are deleted within 30 days, and you will get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and profile picture link.
  • Everything synced to it: reading progress, bookmarks and history, settings, the place used for prayer times, and your adhkar, practices and reminder times.

Nothing is kept after deletion. Photos are never stored, so there are none to delete.

Data on your phone

Deleting your account does not remove the copy of your settings and progress on your phone, so the app keeps working offline. To remove it, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.
